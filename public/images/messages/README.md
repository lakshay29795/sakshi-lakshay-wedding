# Video Message Thumbnails

This folder contains thumbnail images for the video messages page.

## Image Requirements

### Thumbnail Images
- **Size**: Recommended 800x600px (4:3 aspect ratio)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Content**: Photos of your girlfriend with each family member
- **Quality**: High resolution, well-lit, clear faces

### Naming Convention
Use descriptive names that match the video messages:
- `mom-thumbnail.jpg` - Photo with mother
- `dad-thumbnail.jpg` - Photo with father
- `sister-thumbnail.jpg` - Photo with sister
- `brother-thumbnail.jpg` - Photo with brother
- `grandma-thumbnail.jpg` - Photo with grandmother
- `grandpa-thumbnail.jpg` - Photo with grandfather
- `bestfriend-thumbnail.jpg` - Photo with best friend
- etc.

## How to Add Thumbnails

1. Place your photos in this directory (`public/images/messages/`)
2. Name them according to the convention above
3. Update the paths in `src/data/video-messages-data.ts` if needed

## Tips
- Choose photos where both people are clearly visible
- Use recent, high-quality photos
- Ensure good lighting and composition
- Photos should be appropriate for the romantic/wedding theme
